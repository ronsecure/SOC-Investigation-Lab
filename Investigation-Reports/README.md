# SOC Investigation Reports

This section will contain documented security investigation exercises, including evidence review, event timelines, findings, and investigation conclusions.

## Report Structure

Each investigation will include:

- **Alert Overview:** What triggered the investigation?
- **Evidence:** Relevant logs, events, and observations.
- **Timeline:** Sequence of important activities.
- **Analysis:** What does the available evidence indicate?
- **Conclusion:** Benign, suspicious, or requiring escalation.
- **Recommendations:** Suggested next investigative steps.

## Investigation Focus

Building clear, structured investigation reports that demonstrate analytical thinking, evidence-based decisions, and professional documentation.
